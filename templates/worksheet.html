How to use this book

There is no wrong way to use a gentle plan. One worksheet a week counts. One line counts. A page you fill half of and abandon also counts, because a half-plan that exists beats a perfect plan that doesn't.

Tip

Pick the worksheet you most want to do, not the one you most want to have done. Wanting is the fuel here.
